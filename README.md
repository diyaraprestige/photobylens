## What it does
- **40 lenses**, from a Canon 70-200 and a Sony 85 GM to a Leica Noctilux, Helios 44-2 swirl, Trioplan soap-bubble bokeh, anamorphic cinema lenses, fisheye, macro and tilt-shift.
- **AI retouch:** focus point, aperture, white balance, exposure, highlights, shadows and colour, set like a finished edit. It keeps your real photo: nothing is redrawn.
- **Smart framing:** zoom and macro lenses crop in around your subject without cutting off heads or bodies, and you can drag the frame yourself.
- **Free standard download** (1280px). **HD download** at full original resolution, generated automatically after payment.
- **Private:** everything runs on your device. AI uses your own free Gemini key, sent straight from your device to Google.

## Install

### Android
1. On your phone, download **[Photobylens.apk](https://github.com/diyaraprestige/photobylens/releases/latest/download/Photobylens.apk)**.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser.
3. Tap **Install**. Android 7.0 or newer.

### Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera
1. Download **[Photobylens-extension.zip](https://github.com/diyaraprestige/photobylens/releases/latest/download/Photobylens-extension.zip)** and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`) and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder. Click the Photobylens icon in your toolbar to open the studio.

### Web and desktop
Open **https://diyaraprestige.github.io/photobylens/**. In Chrome or Edge, use **Install app** in the address bar to add it to your desktop.

## Connect your free Gemini (optional, recommended)
1. Go to **[aistudio.google.com/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey)** and sign in with your Google account.
2. Click **Create API key** and copy it.
3. In Photobylens, click **Connect free Gemini** and paste it. The key is saved only on your device.

No billing is needed. On Google's free tier, Google may use the data you send to improve its products.
